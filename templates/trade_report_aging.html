How rows are formed: each row is one delivery event. Partial receipts (one event per unique receipt date) and full deliveries (one event on the trade's Mark Complete date for anything the partial receipts didn't cover) are listed separately, so you can chase the right invoice per shipment.

Cash allocation: customer cash payments and any paid-by-customer trade costs that aren't tagged to a specific delivery date are applied FIFO across delivery events ordered by due date — earliest invoice paid first. The Bilty Credit column shows the customer-paid bilty that's specifically tagged to that one delivery, deducted directly from that event's invoice.

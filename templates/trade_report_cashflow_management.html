Cash Flow Management

Smart payment instructions for your existing trades. As each customer receipt lands, it tells you exactly how much to hand each vendor — everyone gets paid, split to favour newer vendors and push long-standing ones. It also projects the upcoming vendor payments as goods still on order arrive (from each vendor's supply history), and flags a recommended capital injection if those payables outrun your incoming cash.
